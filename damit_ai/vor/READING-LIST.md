# Starter library: public dam-safety publications

A suggested first library for `vor`. Download them on your Mac (the cloud workspace can't
reach these agency sites, so none of these links or document numbers were checked from here).
Search the agency's site for the document number. Save the files into `~/Vor/library/`, in
sub-folders if you like, then run `vor ingest`. Add a `library.csv` (see
`library.csv.example`) or run `vor meta` so each citation shows the right title, year
and number.

## Federal guidance (FEMA / ICODS)
- FEMA P-946 (2013): Federal Guidelines for Inundation Mapping of Flood Risks Associated with Dam Incidents and Failures
- FEMA P-94 (2013): Selecting and Accommodating Inflow Design Floods for Dams
- FEMA P-64 (2013): Emergency Action Planning for Dams
- FEMA P-93 (2015): Federal Guidelines for Dam Safety Risk Management
- FEMA 333 (2004): Hazard Potential Classification System for Dams
- FEMA 484 (2005): Technical Manual: Conduits through Embankment Dams
- FEMA P-1014 (2014): Technical Manual: Overtopping Protection for Dams
- FEMA: Filters for Embankment Dams, Best Practices for Design and Construction (2011)

## Reclamation (USBR)
- DSO-98-004, Wahl (1998): Prediction of Embankment Dam Breach Parameters, A Literature Review and Needs Assessment
- DSO-99-06, Graham (1999): A Procedure for Estimating Loss of Life Caused by Dam Failure
- ACER Technical Memorandum No. 11 (1988): Downstream Hazard Classification Guidelines
- RCEM (2015): Reclamation Consequence Estimating Methodology
- Design of Small Dams, 3rd ed. (1987)
- USBR/USACE: Best Practices in Dam and Levee Safety Risk Analysis (current edition)

## USACE, NRCS, FERC
- USACE HEC TD-39 (2014): Using HEC-RAS for Dam Break Studies
- USACE HEC-RAS 2D Modeling User's Manual (the version you run)
- USACE ER 1110-2-1156 (2014): Safety of Dams, Policy and Procedures
- NRCS TR-210-60 (TR-60): Earth Dams and Reservoirs
- NRCS TR-66: Simplified Dam-Breach Routing Procedure
- FERC Engineering Guidelines, Chapter 2 (Selecting and Accommodating Inflow Design Floods) and Chapter 8 (Determination of the PMF)

## North Dakota
- ND State Water Commission: North Dakota Dam Design Handbook
- ND Administrative Code ch. 89-08 (dams, dikes and other devices) and the State Engineer's dam-safety guidance
- NOAA Atlas 14 Volume 8 (precipitation frequency, Midwestern states incl. ND)

## Your own material
Your project notes (for example `breach-parameters/BREACH-PARAMETERS.md` in the project files), run
reports and past studies can go in too. `.md` and `.txt` are indexed by section.
