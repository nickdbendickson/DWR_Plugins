A max pool above the DEM crest puts the maximum at the crest (MAXPOOL-ABOVE-CREST, the fill from the pool scaled to reach it), so the storage at the top of dam (tool 5's Vw, `td_vw_m3`) is never below the inventory maximum, as the old `ensure_top_of_dam` guaranteed; it is set to the maximum, which can be less than the normal storage plus the DEM fill to the crest (the inventory difference replaces that fill, as it does between the two pools). A max pool at the normal pool with a larger maximum is MAXPOOL-ELEV-BAD (the fallback). A normal equal to the maximum is flagged NORMAL-EQUALS-MAX and not used at the pool; records without pool elevations (NID-only, or a tool-1 layer older than 0.17.47) keep the old placement at the top of dam, flagged MAXPOOL-ELEV-MISSING / FALLBACK, where only a normal equal to the maximum is dropped below the pool (a normal above the maximum stays at the pool as before, flagged NORMAL-NOT-BELOW-MAX).
