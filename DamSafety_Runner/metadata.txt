about=Builds the breach hydrograph (ND REG_05.2026 Section 5.1.1: Froehlich 2008/1995, MacDonald, Von Thun & Gillette, TR-60 or your own), optionally routes a PMF inflow with and without the breach, runs LISFLOOD-FP on a local DEM and extends the domain until nothing downstream matters, and can re-run the flood with ANUGA. Writes depth, velocity, arrival and WSE GeoTIFFs plus baseline, incremental, vector and EAP-map products. Includes sensitivity, cascade, nested-grid and Monte Carlo tools, rainfall-to-PMF, life-safety and damage estimates, animation, a Word submittal report, HEC-RAS import/export, a verification suite, a self-test for this QGIS, LiDAR/NLCD data tools and a hand-off to the ND hazard classification. Runs offline. LISFLOOD-FP (patched build) and ANUGA (own conda environment) are installed separately; see README.md.

changelog=3.11.0 (2026-10-28; aerial basemap on QA maps)
    * Slot fill and the dam data read the DWR dams, dam lines, unknown structures and dugouts from the active format-2 pack (dwr/DWR_*.gpkg, built with Hazard 2.18.66 build_nd_pack --dwr-folder) when the suite folder has no DWR file; a DWR file of the engineer's own (suite folder or dwr/) always wins over the pack's copy. No other change.
    * On a dry domain ANUGA has no CFL limit, so the first internal step was the whole 300 s yield step and the inlet's 0.5*(Q(t)+Q(t+dt))*dt booked 0.5*Q(300 s)*300 (DWR-3034: 9,994 m3 for 5,929 m3, G5 ratio 1.0461). While the hydrograph flows, domain.evolve_max_timestep is now the hydrograph interval (median spacing, at most 1/100 of its duration and the yield step, at least 0.5 s); the old limit comes back at the first yield step after the hydrograph ends.
    * NDBREACH_ANUGA_INFLOW_MAX_DT = seconds overrides it; 0 / off = the old behaviour. Recorded in the volume balance (inflow_step_cap).
    * Tests: tests/test_anuga_inflow_step_cap.py (rule, release, and a real-ANUGA bowl: volume ratio 1.0000 capped vs 1.2000 uncapped).
    * G5 note: when the domain gains water, the note now gives the inflow operators' share of the gain (fractional-step integral minus the hydrograph) and, when ANUGA's first yield was one internal step, that step's booked volume against the hydrograph's (volume_balance first_step). DWR-3034: 92,251 m3 added for 88,186 m3, 4,065 m3 = 100% of the gain from one 300 s first step (9,994 m3 booked for 5,929 m3); edge 0. It no longer names the mesh edge or an instability for that case; the edge sentence and caution stay for an edge-driven gain.
