description=Finds dams, dry dams, road fills, ponds, dugouts and road / rail stream crossings in LiDAR, hydrography and aerial imagery that are not on the DWR, NID or bridge / culvert lists.
about=Four Processing tools (Processing Toolbox > DamSafety Finder > Find structures). Fills across streams: walks the 3DHP / NHD flowlines over a bare-earth DEM and reports every fill that blocks the valley (spill level, height, head, storage, pool), as a dam, dry embankment or road fill. Ponds and dugouts: the Screening tool 16 pond engine over a water-body layer. Crossings: every road / rail x stream crossing, the places a culvert or bridge must be. Open water: pond outlines from 4-band or colour-infrared aerial imagery (NDWI), marked when no water-body layer has them. Every candidate is checked against the known-structure layers you give (DWR dams, dugouts, Unknown Structures, NID, NBI, NDDOT, USGS / SARP crossings): listed, unsure or new. The not-listed dams and ponds are also written as DWR-style dam records (status Candidate) that DamSafety Screening tool 1 reads. Screening aid: check every candidate against an aerial image; the Department makes the determination.
