DamSafety Setup 1.0.10 - missing dam height and storage: NID fill, DEM estimate;
not-a-dam records hidden
=================================================================================
(1.0.9 is the ellipsis-only release of #89; this follows it.)

Why
  1.0.8 keeps Next off while a dam's height or storage is 0, so the Runner never gets
  0 ft / 0 ac-ft. Many DWR records have no height or storage (42 of the 74 Cass County
  dams). This version fills what it can from the National Inventory of Dams (NID); typing
  the numbers in stays the fallback.

What changed (dam page)
  * The NID layer in the suite data folder (the offline pack's hydro/NID_dams.gpkg, found
    by the folder scan) fills blanks as soon as the dams are listed. Read-only; no network.
  * "Fill from NID (online)" asks the NID web service (nid.sec.usace.army.mil) for the
    current dam. Only on that click: the wizard makes no network call by itself. It goes
    through QGIS's network settings (proxy), does not freeze the window, gives up after
    15 s and says so; the values can then be typed in.
  * A dam is matched by its NID id (DWR national_id), else by the nearest NID dam within
    100 m of the DWR point.
  * Only blanks are filled. 0 counts as blank (DWR stores "not recorded" as 0). A DWR value
    above 0 is never replaced, and a storage still blank takes the DWR normal pool volume
    before the NID. A value typed in is kept by the online fill.
  * NID height: NID_HEIGHT, else DAM_HEIGHT (ft). NID storage: MAX_STORAGE, else
    NID_STORAGE, else NORMAL_STORAGE (ac-ft).
  * A grey note beside each value says where it came from: "from DWR", "from DWR (normal
    pool volume)", "from NID ND00189", "typed in". The tooltip names the NID field and how
    the dam was matched. The DEM and Run pages show the same.
  * Max storage goes up to 50,000,000 ac-ft (the NID gives 26,000,000 for Snake Creek /
    Lake Sakakawea; the old 5,000,000 cap would have cut it silently).

Hand-off to the Runner
  * The wizard answers carry height_source and storage_source; the Runner parameters carry
    HEIGHT_SOURCE and STORAGE_SOURCE, and the QGIS log ("DamSafety Setup") records them.
    The Runner's processing dialog only reads parameters it defines, so these extra keys
    do not change the run and are not yet written to the Runner's run_report.json.

Dam reader fix
  * A dam with no point (blank or 0, 0) now takes the midpoint of its dam line, transformed
    to EPSG:4326 like the inventory points, and is marked "point from the dam line". The old
    code looked the line up but only set from_line = False and never used it (and only when
    the lines sat in the same GeoPackage, which the pack does not do). The unused
    _line_midpoints helper, which would have replaced every inventory point, is gone.
    Three DWR dams (all Sargent County) have no point; all three have a dam line.

Records that are not a dam to model (dam_filter.py)
  * Hidden from the dam list by default; "Show all records" (off) brings them back, each
    marked "- not a dam? <reason>" and the reason in full under the list. The count line
    says how many were hidden.
  * Hidden when the DWR status is Removed, Breached, Not Built or Submerged (an old dam
    under a newer reservoir), or when the remarks match (case-insensitive):
        ^\W*void\b                       remarks start with VOID ("VOID SEE #80")
        \bno dam (is )?(visible|found|located|present|constructed|built|exist|exists|there)\b
        \bnot a dam\b
        \bdam does not exist\b | \bdoes not exist anymore\b
        \b(did|does) not show ?a dam\b
    A status of Intact, Under Construction or Pending wins over a remark (3 Intact dams
    have an old "no dam visible" remark). "Permit is void" / "permit voided" do not match.
  * Pack (ND_Dam_Suite_Pack, 10,088 records): 1,525 hidden statewide (Breached 1,095,
    Not Built 222, Submerged 83, Removed 73, remarks 52); Cass 24 of 74.

Estimate from DEM (dem_estimate.py)
  * "Estimate from DEM" on the dam page, on when the height or storage is blank. It uses
    the DEM chosen on the DEM page, else one already downloaded for this dam into the
    Dams folder; with neither the button reads "Choose DEM and estimate..." and asks for a
    file (which the DEM page then starts with). The estimate stays on the dam page because
    Next is off there until both values are above 0.
  * Height = crest elevation (median ground along the dam line; without a line the high
    ground within 40 m of the point) minus the lowest ground 2 cells to 150 m downstream.
    The downstream side is the side with the lower ground.
  * Storage = DEM volume below the crest in the basin upstream: a flood fill from the
    lowest ground just upstream, on the upstream side of the dam, inside 1.5, 3, 6 then
    12 km. A fill that reaches the edge of 12 km has no closed basin: no storage. No
    embankment in the DEM (crest not 0.5 m above the toe): neither value.
  * Units: the raster's unit (band unit, vertical CRS: feet / US survey feet / metres)
    wins, else the DEM page's choice, else metres; results in ft and ac-ft. A window wider
    than 1,600 cells is read averaged.
  * Runs in a QgsTask with progress on the page. Fills only blank values; a typed value
    always wins. Labelled "estimated from DEM (screening only)"; height_source /
    storage_source carry the crest, toe, basin area and radius to the hand-off.
  * Screening only: LiDAR sees the water surface, so the storage is the volume above the
    water on the survey day, and a low weir or a dam under water does not show.
  * Copied from DamSafety_Runner (plugins do not import each other): _sample, _flood_fill
    and the reservoir mask (core/hecras_model.py, #79), z_to_m_factor and the unit names
    (core/gdal_io.py).

Tests

  DamSafety_Setup/tests/test_dem_estimate.py: a synthetic DEM with a 200 m x 200 m pit 8 m
  below the crest (259.4 ac-ft) and a 10 m dam (32.8 ft), in metres and in US survey feet;
