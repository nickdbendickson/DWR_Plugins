supportsQt6=True
description=Consolidates dam hazard data and generates ACER-11 hazard classifications (Class 1=High/Red, 2=Judgment/Yellow, 3=Low/Green)
about=Classifies dam hazard under the North Dakota Dam Safety Standards (REG_05.2026) and USBR ACER TM 11. Consolidates NSI / ND GIS Hub address points, POIs and FEMA_Structures building outlines inside the study area (raster extent + 25 ft) against dam-break Depth, Velocity and Arrival rasters (GPKG, CSV, XLSX outputs), joins points to building outlines with 5-ft edge-buffer hazard zones, and generates ACER-11 (USBR TM 11, 1988) hazard classification rasters for houses on foundations and mobile homes.
