    1.5.0 (2026-11-13; security audit pass 3 - bounded zips and a checked NSI download, NO number changes. (1) New damsuite/zipsafe.py: a zip is checked before a byte of it is written - no entry leaves the destination ('..', absolute path, drive letter, symbolic link), at most 200,000 entries, the unpacked size (from the zip's own directory, which Python's zipfile never exceeds) is under the caller's limit and fits on the disk with 256 MB to spare, and an archive over 1 GB is not compressed harder than 1000 to 1 (a 'zip bomb'). Limits: 8 GB for one LiDAR tile, 64 GB for one data set of the updater, a submittal / DSS-WISE package or an offline pack. NDSUITE_ZIP_MAX_GB sets every limit for the rare real archive that is larger. Used by updatecore.extract_zip (before testzip, which reads every entry in full), pack_convert, Screening 0.20.1 and Hazard 2.25.2. (2) NSI structures: USACE publishes no checksum to verify the download against, so the content is checked before it replaces the inventory in use - every structure must be a point with real coordinates inside North Dakota (a few bad records are left out and counted; more than 1 percent of a county, and the answer is refused), one county's answer may not pass 512 MB, and a new inventory with over 20 percent fewer structures than the one in use, or with a county that had structures now empty, is refused and the inventory in use kept (a cut-short answer would silently drop houses from every hazard classification). NDSUITE_NSI_ALLOW_DROP=1 accepts a smaller inventory that is real. nsi_summary.json now records the structure count, the counties with structures, the records left out and the SHA-256 of every API response.)
