    0.17.29 (2026-10-19)
    * Download DEM has a third source, 'USGS 3DEP 1 m lidar tiles (native, TNM)' (core/lidar_tnm.py): the 3DEP 1 m lidar project tiles themselves (10 km GeoTIFFs at 1 m, NAD83 UTM metres, about 275 MB each) from The National Map Access API, not the resampled ImageServer image. Area: a box (BBOX), else a dam folder (DAM_FOLDER: the Runner's model bounds plus DOMAIN_PAD_M, else dam.json's point and the buffer), else the dam point and buffer, else the county; never the state. The first run lists the tiles (project, collection and publication dates, size), the total, what is still to download and the free disk space, and downloads nothing; LIDAR_DOWNLOAD downloads them (refused over LIDAR_MAX_GB, default 10, or when less than 2 GB would stay free). Tiles go to TILE_FOLDER, else <suite data folder>/dem/lidar_1m/, else <output>/lidar_1m (never the offline pack or a read-only folder); tiles already there are skipped, a cut download resumes from its .part (HTTP Range), each tile is retried 4 times and checked (size, GDAL opens it at 1 m with a CRS), and lidar_1m_manifest.json records tile, project, dates, URL, sha256 and bytes. Where two projects publish the same tile the newest collection is used and the log says so (LIDAR_KEEP_OLDER keeps the older one underneath). The result is <DAM_ID>_1m_lidar.vrt in the output folder (1 m, the tiles' UTM zone, newest collection on top, the coarser DEM underneath: FALLBACK_DEM, else <DAM_ID>_10m_5070.tif when it is there), <DAM_ID>_1m_lidar.json (projects, tiles, dates, fallback) and the _dem.txt facts.
